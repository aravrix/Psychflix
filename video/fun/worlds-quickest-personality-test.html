World's Quickest Personality Test

This video presents a quick personality test based on the Big Five model of personality, which measures openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. The presenter, Richard Wiseman, is a British psychologist, professor, author, and researcher known for his work on psychology, luck, self-development, and human behavior. Through his Quirkology project, he shares psychological concepts and experiments in an engaging and accessible way, helping viewers understand the science behind everyday thoughts and actions.
