Experts Apply DSM-5 to Real Patient with Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia is a severe psychological disorder characterized by disturbances in thinking, perception, emotions, and behavior. Common symptoms include hallucinations, delusions, disorganized speech, and difficulties with daily functioning. In this clinical case, mental health experts apply the DSM-5 diagnostic criteria to evaluate a real patient with schizophrenia, demonstrating how symptoms are assessed and how effective treatment can lead to significant improvement in quality of life.
