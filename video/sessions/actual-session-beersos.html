An Actual Therapy Session | Beersos #179

This episode features a real therapy session led by licensed therapist Aaron Johnstone. During the conversation, Derek explores experiences from his upbringing, discusses how past events have influenced his thoughts and emotions, and works with the therapist to develop healthier perspectives and coping strategies. The session provides an accessible look at the therapeutic process, demonstrating how professional therapy can help individuals understand their experiences, process difficult emotions, and work toward positive personal growth.
