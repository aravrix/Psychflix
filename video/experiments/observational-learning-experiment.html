Bandura and Observational Learning - Experiment

Observational learning, also known as social learning, is the process of learning by watching and imitating the behavior of others. Psychologist Albert Bandura demonstrated this concept through his famous Bobo Doll experiments, which showed that children can learn aggressive and non-aggressive behaviors simply by observing models. This experiment became a cornerstone of social learning theory and highlighted the powerful role of observation in shaping human behavior.
