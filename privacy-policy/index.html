Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Psychflix handles information when you visit our curated psychology video directory. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

1. Information when you browse

You can browse the public video directory without creating a Psychflix account. The site does not ask you to provide your name, address or phone number to view its catalogue. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and embedded third-party services may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, request logs and security signals as part of delivering and protecting the website. Their own privacy policies apply to their processing.

2. Saved videos on this device

If you choose Local Storage, your saved video IDs and storage preference are stored in your browser's local storage on that device. This data is not automatically synced to our servers and may be lost if you clear site data or change browsers/devices.

3. Google sign-in and Google Drive

Psychflix may provide optional Google sign-in and Google Drive syncing for saved videos. If you choose to connect Google, Google will display the permissions requested and you decide whether to grant them. The planned Drive integration uses the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which is intended to let Psychflix create and manage only the specific file(s) used by the app, rather than access all files in your Drive.

If and when Drive syncing is enabled, Psychflix will use Google account identification needed to associate your connection, an authorization token needed to maintain access, and the Psychflix saved-video list (video identifiers and related sync metadata). The saved list is stored in your own Google Drive. If a backend connection is used to maintain authorization, refresh-token credentials will be stored securely by the service and used only to renew access for the requested Psychflix Drive feature. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We do not access unrelated Drive files.

Drive connection is optional. You can continue using local storage instead. You can disconnect or revoke Psychflix's access from your Google Account security settings. Removing access may stop syncing; you can also delete the Psychflix saved-list file from your Drive. Google independently processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Feature status: Google Drive syncing is being planned/developed and may not yet be available. This section describes the intended data handling if the feature is released; the live implementation and requested permissions will be disclosed before you authorize it.

4. Embedded videos and external links

Psychflix is a curated directory. Videos are generally embedded from or linked to third-party platforms rather than uploaded or hosted by Psychflix. When you load an embed, the provider (for example, YouTube) may receive information about your visit, use cookies or similar technologies, and process data under its own privacy policy. You can avoid this processing by not playing or loading third-party embeds where the interface allows, and by using the provider's privacy controls.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Psychflix does not currently intend to use advertising cookies or serve ads. Hosting, embedded media and other third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. In the future, we may consider a small amount of minimal advertising to help cover costs. If advertising is introduced, we will update this policy to explain the providers and data practices before or when the feature is launched.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host, secure and operate Psychflix, or disclosed where required by law. Local saved-video data remains in your browser until you remove it or clear browser data. Google Drive data remains in your Drive until you delete it. Any account or authorization data processed by a future backend will be retained only as needed to provide the connection, meet security requirements and comply with applicable law; exact retention and deletion controls will be described when that system launches.

7. Children's privacy and age guidance

Psychflix is recommended for users aged 13 and older. Some psychology material may include references to sexuality or sexual innuendo, including in discussions of historical theories. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to Psychflix, please contact us through the project's published contact or issue-reporting options so we can review it.

8. Your choices

  • Use the site without signing in.
  • Choose local storage instead of Google Drive.
  • Decline Google permissions or revoke them later in your Google Account.
  • Clear local browser storage to remove locally saved video IDs.
  • Delete the Psychflix saved-list file from your Google Drive if Drive syncing is enabled.

9. Open project and content rights

Psychflix's source code is publicly available in our GitHub repository. Psychflix is a curated directory and does not claim ownership of third-party embedded videos or linked materials. Those materials remain subject to their respective owners' rights and platform policies. Public source availability does not transfer ownership of third-party content.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features and providers change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact or issue-reporting options published on the Psychflix website or its GitHub repository.
